A canyon made of code.

A 150-second journey from the rim to the river and back into the light. Drag gently to look around, or use the timeline to find your own view.

Every landform, rock layer, cloud, water ripple, and sound is generated mathematically. No photographs, external models, libraries, or network connection are used.

This is an artistic reconstruction inspired by the Grand Canyon, not a surveyed route.
